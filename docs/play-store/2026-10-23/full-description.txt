Rakky Ride brings passenger rides and small parcel deliveries together in one app. Choose your addresses, review the fare and follow your trip from pickup to arrival.

BOOK A RIDE
Set your pickup and destination, compare available ride options and review your estimated fare before requesting a driver. Follow the trip on the map and view past trips and receipts.

PLAN AHEAD
Schedule a pickup from 30 minutes to 7 days in advance. Manage upcoming bookings from the Schedule screen. Driver search starts close to pickup time; a driver is confirmed only after accepting your request.

SEND A PARCEL
Arrange delivery of a sealed package weighing up to 5 kg. Add the recipient's details and delivery notes, choose collection and delivery addresses, and review the fare. Pay from your wallet and share the delivery handoff code with your recipient. Prohibited items are not accepted, and packages may be inspected.

MANAGE PAYMENTS AND TRIPS
Use cash or wallet for eligible passenger rides, manage your wallet and review transaction and trip history. Courier deliveries are paid from the sender's wallet.

DRIVE WITH RAKKY RIDE
Apply to become a driver and manage your vehicle and documents in the same app. Once approved, choose when to go online and which approved services to receive. Car drivers can apply for passenger rides and courier deliveries; motorcycle drivers can apply for courier deliveries. Track trips and earnings, manage your bank account and request withdrawals.

HELP WHEN YOU NEED IT
Find trip assistance, safety information and support options from the app. Manage your profile, location settings and notifications in one place.

Services and ride options depend on your area and driver availability. Scheduled bookings do not guarantee a driver in advance. Drivers must complete the relevant application and approval process before receiving requests.
